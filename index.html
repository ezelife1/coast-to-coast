A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

How often should driveways be cleaned?

It depends on the surface, shade and how quickly dirt and growth return. Send a photo and we can help you decide whether your driveway is ready for a clean.

Do you offer soft washing?

Yes. Soft washing is available for suitable exterior surfaces. We assess the surface before recommending a cleaning method.

Is pressure washing suitable for every surface?

The cleaning approach depends on the material and its condition. We discuss the right method for your paving, render or other exterior surface before work begins.

Do you cover Exmouth and East Devon?

Yes. We are based in Exmouth and cover Exeter and the surrounding East Devon towns listed above. Contact us if you are unsure whether your property is within our area.

How long will the clean take?

Timing depends on the size, condition and access at the property. We can give an estimate when preparing your quote.

Do I need to provide water access?

Let us know what outside water access is available when you enquire, so we can plan the job with you.

How do I get a quote?

Call us, send photos on WhatsApp or complete the form below. Quotes are free and carry no obligation.
